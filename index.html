AVO Music · iOS

Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026 · Developer: Murodilbek

At a glance

AVO Music is an offline music player. Your music, playlists and settings stay on your device. The app has no accounts, servers, ads or analytics.

  • No account. You never sign up or log in.
  • No data collection. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your personal data.
  • No tracking, ads or analytics. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • One optional network feature. If you tap "Search Lyrics Online", the song's title, artist, album and length are sent to the public lyrics service LRCLIB (lrclib.net). Nothing is sent otherwise.
  • You are in control. Delete app data from Settings, or delete the app.

Data stored on your device

AVO Music keeps the following only inside the app's storage on your device. The app never uploads it to us or to anyone else.

Permissions

  • Media & Apple Music. Requested only when you choose "Import from Apple Music". The app reads the songs you select to copy them into AVO Music. It does not read your library in the background and cannot import DRM-protected tracks. You can withdraw access in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Media & Apple Music.
  • Files. Used only through the iOS file picker, which gives the app access to the audio files you pick, or files you open in the app from elsewhere.
  • Background audio. Music keeps playing when the app is in the background or the screen is locked. This is playback only.
  • AirPlay and Bluetooth. Used to send sound to the device you choose. iOS handles device selection.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or advertising identifier.

Online lyrics search

This is the app's only network feature. Lyrics are off by default (Settings > Lyrics). A request is made only when you turn lyrics on and tap the search button for a song.

The app then sends the following to LRCLIB, a free public lyrics database at lrclib.net, over HTTPS:

  • the song title and artist name;
  • the album name, if known;
  • the song length in seconds, if known;
  • a search phrase you typed, if you search manually;
  • your device's IP address and a generic app identifier ("AVO Music iOS/1.0"), which any internet request reveals.

The app does not send your audio files, library, playlists, listening history or any identifier of you. LRCLIB is run by a third party under its own terms and practices, which we do not control. Returned lyrics are saved on your device so they can be shown offline. If you never use this feature, the app makes no network requests.

Tracking, ads, analytics and third parties

AVO Music does not track you across other companies' apps or websites and does not use the advertising identifier. It contains no ads, analytics, crash-reporting, social-login or other third-party SDKs. We do not sell, rent or share personal data, and we do not use data for profiling or marketing.

The only third-party services involved are those built into iOS (media library, Files, AirPlay), governed by Apple's policies, and LRCLIB, described above.

Retention and deletion

Everything is stored locally, so you decide how long it is kept.

  • Clear the library: Settings > Clear Music Library removes your songs, imported audio files, lyrics, play counts and recently played history.
  • Remove single items: delete songs, playlists, favorites or lyrics individually inside the app.
  • Delete everything: deleting the app removes all its data from your device. Copies in your own backups follow your backup settings.
  • Data at LRCLIB: we hold none. Requests made to LRCLIB cannot be deleted through the app; contact LRCLIB directly.

Children

AVO Music does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the age set by local law). The app has no accounts, ads or social features. Its only online feature is the lyrics search described above.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. Everything the app stores is on your device and you can view or remove it yourself. If you are in the EEA, UK, California or another region with privacy rights, you can still contact us with any question and we will answer it.

Security

Data stays in the app's sandboxed storage protected by iOS, and the lyrics request uses encrypted HTTPS. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so keep your device updated and locked.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, for example a new feature that uses the network, we will update this policy and its date before the change ships.
